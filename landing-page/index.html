Who we are

We show up on the same streets, week after week.

Paw Rescue is a small, stubbornly consistent team. We go wherever animals need help — feeding the regulars, checking on the injured, and carrying the ones who can't wait until morning. No stray is "someone else's problem." They're ours.

What we do every day

Four ways we show up.

Feeding Drives

Fixed routes, every day, rain or heat. Regular meals mean fewer strays go hungry or wander into traffic.

Emergency Rescue

A 24/7 helpline for injured or trapped animals. Real humans, day or night, ready to come out.

Medical Aid

Mobile first-aid kits and a vet on call. Wounds get cleaned the same night, not whenever a clinic opens.

Adoption Help

Gentle rehabilitation, behavior support, and careful matching with families who'll love them back.
